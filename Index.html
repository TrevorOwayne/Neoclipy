THE THESIS

1 · Problem

A barber in Abuja has no verifiable income history. Cash leaves no record. Nigeria's unbanked rate sits around 17%, concentrated in this population.

2 · Why not a database

A private ledger controlled by Neo-Clip isn't portable to an external lender. The record has to be independently verifiable.

3 · Why Solana

Solana processed $650B in stablecoin transactions in Feb 2026 and carries ~46% of stablecoin transfers among peer chains. Sub-cent settlement is the only way micro-payments at ₦500 work.
